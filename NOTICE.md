**Audex Revival** is an independent reimplementation of **Audex: Accessibility
Redefined** (`in.delight.Audex`), originally published in 2017 by Akash Kakkar
(Akash Delight Computings).

The audio files under `revival/app/src/main/res/raw/` and the launcher icon under
`revival/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/` are taken from the original Audex APK. They
remain the property of their original creator and are included only so the
accessibility behavior can be preserved for existing users. Remove or replace them
if you do not have the right to redistribute them.

The original application's Firebase configuration, anti-piracy routine, privileged
auto-enable code, and dead service URLs are not included.

The original APK and the raw decompiled Xamarin/apktool output are not included in
this repository.
